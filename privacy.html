Privacy Policy

Rock • Paper • Scissors Championship

Effective date: 19 November 2026

1. About this policy

Rock • Paper • Scissors Championship (“the Game”) is developed and operated by Nathan Morley, an individual developer based in Australia.

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game, visit its website or contact support.

For privacy questions, contact rpschampionship.support@pocketglazier.com.au.

2. Offline gameplay and local information

The initial release provides single-player gameplay against the Challenger and local two-player gameplay on one iPhone. These modes do not require an internet connection or a Game account.

The Game stores information locally on your device, including:

  • Solo championship results, wins, losses and winning streaks.
  • Names or nicknames used for same-phone two-player rivalry records.
  • Local rivalry results and statistics.
  • Music, sound-effects and haptic preferences.

Player names are optional. You can use the default names or nicknames instead of real names.

Current matches and selections are also processed locally to operate the Game. An unfinished championship is not guaranteed to resume after the app closes.

3. Information collected through the Game

The initial release does not automatically transmit gameplay records or player names to Nathan Morley or a developer-operated server.

It does not include advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDKs, and does not use advertising identifiers or track activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

The cinematic videos and audio are included with the app and play locally. Playing them does not upload player information to an AI generation service.

The initial release does not offer network multiplayer, developer-managed cloud saves or online player accounts.

4. Apple services and backups

Apple may independently process information associated with the App Store, device operation, backups and diagnostics according to Apple’s policies and your settings.

Local application data may be included in device or computer backups. The Game does not provide its own cloud synchronisation service.

5. Support correspondence

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, your message and any information or attachments you choose to provide. These may include your device model, iOS version, screenshots and a description of a problem.

The developer uses this information to respond, investigate reported issues, provide support and handle privacy enquiries. Support information is not sold or used for third-party advertising.

Email and related service providers may process correspondence to deliver and operate the support service. Information may be processed or stored outside Australia, depending on the providers involved.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Website hosting

The Game’s website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process other technical information under its own privacy practices. This website activity is separate from the Game’s offline gameplay.

See GitHub’s privacy statement.

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records generally remain until the app or its application data is removed. Deleting the app generally removes its local data. Offloading the app may preserve that data.

Deleting the app does not delete copies retained in backups or support correspondence already sent to the developer.

The Game does not currently provide a general in-app record-deletion control. The developer cannot remotely access or delete records held only on your device. You can manage your device and backup settings through the relevant services.

Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle enquiries, maintain relevant support records and meet applicable legal obligations. No fixed retention period is promised.

You may contact the developer to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held by the developer through support correspondence. Applicable legal requirements or legitimate recordkeeping needs may limit deletion.

8. Children’s privacy

Offline gameplay does not require an account, email address or real name.

The Game does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its gameplay features. Parents and guardians may contact the developer about information voluntarily provided through support.

The App Store age rating describes content suitability. It should not be interpreted as a guarantee that the Game is suitable for every child.

9. Security and sharing

Local records use standard iOS application storage mechanisms. Device access and backup settings affect their protection.

Support information is handled through the services used to operate the support mailbox. It may also be disclosed where required by law.

No electronic storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Advertising, purchases and policy changes

The initial release is free and contains no advertising, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

If features or data-handling practices change, this policy and applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be reviewed and updated. Additional notice or choices will be provided where required.

The current policy will be available through the Game and its published website. The effective date will identify the current version.
