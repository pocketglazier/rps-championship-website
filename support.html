Support

Rock • Paper • Scissors Championship

For help, questions or feedback, email rpschampionship.support@pocketglazier.com.au.

Gameplay and compatibility

The Game supports iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later.

Play solo against the Challenger, or share one iPhone for a local two-player championship. Neither mode requires an internet connection or a Game account.

The initial release is free, with no advertising, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Troubleshooting

If you encounter a problem:

  1. Close and reopen the Game.
  2. Check for available app and iOS updates.
  3. Check that your iPhone has sufficient available storage.
  4. If the issue continues, contact support.

For sound problems, check the Game’s Music and Sound Effects settings, your device volume and audio output. The home-screen preview is intentionally silent. The Sound Effects setting also controls mixed audio in fight videos and the full story.

Avoid deleting the app as your first troubleshooting step: locally saved records may be lost. An unfinished championship may not resume after closing the app.

Reporting a problem

Please include:

  • Your iPhone model.
  • Your iOS version.
  • The Game’s version and build number, shown in Settings.
  • What happened and the steps leading up to it.
  • Whether you were playing solo, same-phone two-player or watching the story.

Screenshots are optional. Remove personal information before sending them.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
